Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

WOHO Studios respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information associated with users of our games, applications, websites and online services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Who We Are

WOHO Studios is an independent video game development studio. This Privacy Policy applies to games, applications and online services published or operated by WOHO Studios that link to this policy.

For privacy-related questions or requests, you may contact us at:

Email: wohostudios@gmail.com

Website: https://wohostudios.com

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used in our games or applications, we may process limited information necessary to provide online functionality.

This may include:

  • Epic Account identifier.
  • Epic Games display name.
  • Information necessary to authenticate a user through Epic Account Services.
  • Online presence and multiplayer session information.
  • Information necessary to create, discover or join multiplayer sessions or lobbies.
  • Technical information required to operate and troubleshoot online game services.

The specific information available to WOHO Studios depends on the Epic Online Services features and permissions enabled for the applicable game.

3. Epic Online Services

Our games may use Epic Online Services ("EOS"), which is provided by Epic Games, Inc. and its affiliates.

Epic Online Services may provide authentication, multiplayer, lobby, matchmaking, social or other online functionality.

When you choose to sign in using your Epic Games account, Epic Games may provide our application with information authorized through Epic Account Services, such as your unique Epic Account identifier and display name.

WOHO Studios uses Epic Account information only for purposes related to operating the game and its online functionality, including authentication, multiplayer functionality and permitted social features.

We do not use Epic Account information for unrelated advertising or data brokerage purposes.

Epic Games separately processes information according to its own privacy practices. You can review Epic Games' privacy policy at:

Epic Games Privacy Policy

4. How We Use Information

Information processed through our games and online services may be used to:

  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide online multiplayer functionality.
  • Create and manage multiplayer sessions and lobbies.
  • Allow players to connect with other players.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of our services.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve functionality.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Epic Account data is used only for purposes permitted by Epic Account Services and applicable law.

5. Information We Do Not Request

WOHO Studios does not request your Epic Games password.

Authentication credentials such as your Epic Games password are handled directly by Epic Games through its authentication systems.

We also do not sell or rent Epic Account information to advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to provide features requested by users. For example, Epic Games processes information in connection with Epic Online Services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights or safety of our users, services or others.

7. Data Retention

WOHO Studios retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the applicable service, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal requirements, or resolve disputes.

Some information associated with Epic Online Services may be retained and processed by Epic Games according to Epic's own policies and agreements.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

However, no online service or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may provide you with rights concerning your personal information. These may include rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of certain processing.

To submit a privacy-related request concerning information controlled by WOHO Studios, contact:

wohostudios@gmail.com

Requests concerning information controlled directly by Epic Games should be submitted to Epic Games through its applicable privacy or account support channels.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner that requires parental consent and such consent has not been obtained, we will take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law.

11. International Users

Online services may operate internationally, and information may be processed in countries other than the country in which you reside.

Where required, we take steps intended to handle such information in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our games, services or legal obligations change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
